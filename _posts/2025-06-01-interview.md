Preparing for a technical writer interview involves understanding both the role and your own experience. Below are some commonly asked questions and sample answers based on my background.

---

## 1. Tell me about yourself

I have a strong background in English Interpretation and Business English, with a Master’s degree from the University of International Business and Economics and a Bachelor’s from Jinan University. Since 2020, I have worked as a Technical Writer at Espressif Systems, where I collaborate with engineers to develop and maintain technical documentation for hardware products. My experience also includes leading translation teams, managing terminology, and automating documentation workflows.

---

## 2. Walk me through your resume

- **Education:**  
  - Master in English Interpretation, University of International Business and Economics  
  - Bachelor in Business English, Jinan University

- **Work Experience:**  
  - Technical Writer at Espressif Systems (2020–Present)  
    - Developed and maintained hardware documentation (TRMs, datasheets, design guides)
    - Led the ESP-IDF Translation Team, managing over 400 documents across nine chip series
    - Standardized terminology and automated glossary generation
    - Automated translation request workflows and formatting checks
    - Analyzed customer feedback to improve documentation quality

- **Skills:**  
  - Documentation tools: LaTeX, reStructuredText, Markdown  
  - Programming: Python, Linux Shell  
  - Version control: Git  
  - Office tools: Word, Excel, PPT

---

## 3. What is your greatest strength?

My greatest strength is my ability to bridge the gap between technical experts and end users. I excel at translating complex technical concepts into clear, user-friendly documentation. Additionally, I am proactive in automating workflows and standardizing processes to improve team efficiency.

---

## 4. Why should we hire you?

You should hire me because I bring a combination of strong language skills, technical understanding, and process improvement experience. I have a proven track record of delivering high-quality documentation, leading teams, and implementing automation to streamline documentation processes.

---

## 5. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

In five years, I hope to take on greater responsibilities, possibly leading a documentation or content team. I aim to further develop my technical skills and contribute to building best-in-class documentation systems that support both users and engineers.

---

## 6. Why are you leaving your current job?

I am looking for new challenges and opportunities to grow. While I have learned a lot at Espressif Systems, I am eager to expand my experience in a new environment and contribute to a different team with fresh perspectives.

---

## 7. How do you handle stress?

I handle stress by staying organized, prioritizing tasks, and breaking down complex projects into manageable steps. I also communicate proactively with my team to address issues early and ensure deadlines are met without compromising quality.

---

## 8. Describe a difficult work situation and how you overcame it

Once, I managed a large-scale translation project with tight deadlines and frequent updates. By automating the request and tracking process, and by standardizing terminology, I was able to coordinate the team efficiently and deliver high-quality translations on time.

---

## 9. What are your salary expectations?

Based on my experience and the industry standards, I am seeking a competitive salary that reflects my skills and contributions. I am open to discussing the specifics based on the responsibilities and expectations of the role.

---

**Good luck with your interview preparation!**
